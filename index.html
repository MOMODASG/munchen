Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Are the rules the same in every country?+

No. Laws differ by country and may distinguish possession, use, sale, transport and importation. Check current official information for your location.

What effects can THC have?+

Effects vary by person, amount and method of use. THC can impair attention, memory, coordination and judgment. Do not drive or operate machinery while impaired.

Can cannabis pose health risks?+

Yes. Effects may include anxiety, confusion, a faster heart rate or impaired coordination. Risks depend on the person and the product. A healthcare professional can provide advice.

Can THC be detected in a drug test?+

Yes. Some tests look for THC or its metabolites. Detection time varies with several factors, so no exact timeframe can be guaranteed.

What should I do in a serious emergency?+

For breathing difficulty, loss of consciousness, seizures, chest pain or severe distress, contact local emergency services immediately.
